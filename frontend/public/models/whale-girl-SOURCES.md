# Whale Girl (鲸鱼娘)

The model, costume and portrait adapt the supplied “Whale Girl – Chibi 3D Model Reference”.
Character concept by 上善无形. DeepSeek-themed fan refinement by ZipZipPipe.
This is an unofficial fan character. The supplied reference identifies its license as
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Caelis Bot contributors created the 3D adaptation, including the fitted Q-style body,
face, blue-violet hair, maid outfit, whale fins and tail, five animated performances
and the rendered portrait. Version 1.26.0 revises the neckline, arm proportions, hand
orientation and animated mouth. The contributors license this adaptation, including
their Caelis geometry contributions as used here, under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.
Third-party rights remain with their respective holders.
