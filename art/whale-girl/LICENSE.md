# Whale Girl

The Whale Girl character, maid outfit, rendered portrait, Blender model and
viewer reference crop are an unofficial adaptation of the supplied “Whale Girl –
Chibi 3D Model Reference” (鲸鱼娘.png). The reference credits 上善无形 for the
character concept and ZipZipPipe for the DeepSeek-themed fan refinement and
identifies its license as [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Caelis Bot contributors adapted the design into 3D with a fitted body and FK
skeleton, shaped the face and hair, modeled the fins, tail, outfit and footwear,
painted the materials, and authored five animated performances. Version 1.26.0
adjusts the neckline, arm lengths, palms and animated smile. The contributors
make their rights in this adaptation, including their contributions to the Caelis
geometry as incorporated here, available under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.
Third-party rights remain with their respective holders.

The [distribution notice](../../frontend/public/models/whale-girl-SOURCES.md) accompanies the
finished assets. The standalone Caelis character has its [own license](../../ASSET-LICENSE.md).
Modeling scripts and viewer code are [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE).
